一、我哋係邊個

NORVA 諾帆（下稱「我哋」）係一項喺香港營運嘅貸款轉介（中介）服務，由營運者以個人名義經營。我哋幫你比較同接洽持牌財務機構嘅貸款方案。

我哋唔會就任何貸款申請向你收取任何費用——冇手續費、冇保證金、冇按金。我哋嘅收入嚟自財務機構嘅轉介佣金。

本政策解釋我哋透過 Instagram、Facebook Messenger、WhatsApp 等訊息渠道收集你嘅個人資料時，會收咩、點用、存喺邊、留幾耐，同你有咩權利。

二、我哋收集咩資料

2.1 你透過社交平台聯絡我哋時，平台會提供：

  • 你嘅帳戶名稱（handle）同公開顯示名稱
  • 平台專屬嘅用戶識別編號（例如 Instagram-scoped User ID）
  • 你嘅公開頭像
  • 你發送嘅訊息內容、附件同時間
  • 你喺我哋帖文下嘅公開留言（如你留言）

2.2 初步查詢階段，你自願向我哋提供嘅（你可以唔答）：

  • 稱呼、聯絡電話
  • 就業狀況、行業、年資、出糧方式同收入
  • 現有貸款、信用卡及其他負債狀況
  • 環聯（TU，即 TransUnion 信貸資料庫）信貸報告嘅內容或評分
  • 其他你主動講、同貸款申請有關嘅資料

2.3 決定正式申請之後，我哋先會要求嘅身分及證明文件：

如果你決定經我哋向財務機構提交申請，機構會要求以下文件。我哋只會喺呢個階段先向你收取，初步查詢階段唔會要：

  • 香港身份證號碼及副本，或其他身份證明文件
  • 最近三個月住址證明
  • 最近三個月入息證明；個別財務機構或個案可能要求更多月份
  • 工作證明，例如公司證明信、糧單、税單、強積金（MPF，即強制性公積金）供款紀錄
  • 供貸款過數用嘅銀行户口資料
  • 如你申請嘅係信用卡結餘轉戶或清卡數：相關信用卡嘅資料（包括卡號），用嚟指定要清嘅户口
  • 如你申請嘅係物業按揭：差餉單、管理費單、現有物業按揭合約及其他物業相關文件
  • 財務機構就個別申請要求嘅其他文件

呢啲文件只會用嚟提交你嘅貸款申請，用完按第六節嘅保存期處理。

⚠️ 我哋永遠唔會問你攞呢啲：

  • 網上銀行、電子錢包或任何帳戶嘅登入密碼
  • 一次性驗證碼（OTP，即一次性短訊驗證碼）
  • 信用卡背面嘅 CVV／CVC 保安碼、提款卡或信用卡嘅 密碼（PIN）

⚠️ 我哋亦永遠唔會問你收取任何費用。

如果有人用 NORVA 名義問你攞錢、攞密碼或者 OTP，佢唔係我哋。請即刻停止對話並通知我哋。

三、我哋攞嚟做咩（收集目的）

  • 回覆你嘅查詢同保持聯絡
  • 初步評估你嘅情況，幫你篩選比較有機會批核嘅財務機構
  • 經獲正式委任嘅轉介安排，代你向持牌財務機構提交或接洽貸款申請
  • 跟進你嘅申請進度
  • 保存必要記錄，以符合法律及佣金結算需要
  • 只會喺你同意之後：日後有減息方案嘅時候通知你，或者幫你睇信貸狀況有冇改善（詳見第六、九節）

我哋只會收集同上述目的直接相關嘅資料，唔會攞多過需要。

四、我哋會將你嘅資料轉交邊類人

為咗處理你嘅貸款申請，我哋可能會將你嘅個人資料轉交俾以下類別嘅接收者：

  • 香港持牌放債人及財務機構，以及其獲正式委任嘅轉介夥伴——用作評估你嘅貸款申請
  • 法律或監管機構——喺法律要求或法院命令下
  • 為我哋提供技術服務嘅供應商——包括海外人工智能服務供應商同訊息通知服務——只會為處理你嘅查詢而經手你嘅資料

轉交只會喺處理你申請所必需嘅範圍內進行。我哋唔會將你嘅個人資料出售、租借或轉讓俾任何人作促銷或其他無關用途。

五、你係咪一定要俾資料

唔係，全部自願。你可以隨時唔答任何問題、或者唔繼續。

但要老實講：如果你唔提供同你財務狀況相關嘅基本資料，我哋就冇辦法幫你判斷邊間機構有機會批，亦冇辦法代你接洽——即係我哋可能幫唔到你。呢個係唯一後果，唔會有其他影響。

六、資料存喺邊、留幾耐

階段做法
訊息傳送途中經 Meta（Instagram／Facebook／WhatsApp）嘅伺服器傳送，受 Meta 自己嘅私隱政策管轄
我哋嘅伺服器唔長期保存。訊息一經處理即轉走，伺服器唔留底；未處理得切嘅（例如語音訊息）最多留 7 日就自動清走
長期保存只存放喺營運者自己控制嘅裝置及加密雲端儲存，設有存取限制
保存期（冇成事）個案完結後保存 24 個月，之後刪除
保存期（成功批出貸款）交易及佣金紀錄（你嘅稱呼、經手機構、貸款金額、佣金同日期）按《稅務條例》第 51C 條保存最少 7 年；其他資料同上，個案完結後 24 個月刪除
信貸報告唔長期保留報告原件。成功批出貸款嘅，喺回佣期（一般 3 個月）完結後刪除；冇成事嘅，個案完結後刪除

揀 24 個月係因為《放債人條例》第 29(3) 條嘅檢控時效係 2 年，同埋佣金結算可能有爭議需要查證。過咗呢段時間，我哋冇正當理由再保留你嘅資料。成功批出貸款嘅交易及佣金紀錄要留 7 年，係因為《稅務條例》要求保存足夠核實收入嘅紀錄；呢個要求唔包括你嘅身分證明文件副本或者信貸報告原件。

如果我哋停止營運呢項服務，會刪除所有透過平台取得嘅資料。

七、資料保安

  • 只有營運者本人可以接觸你嘅資料——冇其他員工。第四節講嘅技術服務供應商會經手部分資料（例如 AI 要睇到訊息內容先覆到你）
  • 交俾 AI 處理嘅訊息，會先喺我哋自己嘅電腦遮咗你嘅電話、身分證、信用卡同銀行戶口號碼
  • 儲存裝置及雲端帳戶設有密碼及雙重認證
  • 敏感個人資料唔會存放喺任何公開或共用嘅位置

我哋會採取切實可行嘅步驟保護你嘅資料，但要誠實講：冇任何電子傳送或儲存方式係 100% 安全嘅。

八、自動化處理同 AI

我哋會用自動化程式（包括人工智能輔助）接收、整理同回覆訊息，令回覆快啲。

人工智能服務由海外供應商提供，你嘅訊息內容會傳去佢哋嘅伺服器處理。我哋已經關閉「用你資料訓練 AI 模型」嘅選項；佢哋一般最多保留 30 日。

但所有關於你貸款申請嘅實質判斷——包括建議邊間機構、係咪繼續——一律由真人做，唔會由機器單獨決定。你隨時可以要求同真人直接對話。

九、直接促銷

未經你同意，我哋唔會用你嘅個人資料做直接促銷。

具體即係：我哋唔會將你加入任何推廣名單、唔會群發推廣訊息，亦唔會將你嘅資料提供俾第三方作直接促銷用途。

唯一要你親口同意嘅情況：正式申請之前，我哋會問你「日後有更啱你嘅方案（例如減息），可唔可以 WhatsApp 通知你？」只有你答「好」，我哋先會用你嘅稱呼同 WhatsApp 電話號碼，喺日後有減息方案（即係息口比你而家筆貸款更低嘅方案）嘅時候通知你。你隨時講一聲「唔想收」（WhatsApp、Instagram 私訊或者電郵都得，唔使任何費用），我哋就即刻停。

除此之外，我哋只會回覆你主動發起嘅對話，或者跟進你已經開始咗嘅申請。

如果將來我哋想用你嘅資料推廣其他嘢，會事先另行通知你、清楚列明會用邊類資料同推廣咩，並且要攞到你嘅明確同意先會做——依《個人資料（私隱）條例》第 6A 部辦事。

English Version

1. Who we are

NORVA ("we", "us") is a loan referral (intermediary) service operating in Hong Kong, run by an individual operator. We help you compare and approach licensed financial institutions for loan products.

We never charge borrowers any fee — no handling fee, no deposit, no security. Our income comes from referral commissions paid by financial institutions.

This policy explains what personal data we collect when you contact us through Instagram, Facebook Messenger or WhatsApp, how we use it, where it is kept, how long we keep it, and what rights you have.

2. What data we collect

Provided by the platform when you message us: your account handle and public display name; a platform-scoped user identifier (e.g. Instagram-scoped User ID); your public profile picture; the content, attachments and timestamps of your messages; and your public comments on our posts if you leave any.

Provided voluntarily by you at the initial enquiry stage: your preferred name and contact number; employment status, industry, length of service, salary payment method and income; existing loans, credit cards and other liabilities; the contents or score of your TransUnion (TU) credit report; and any other information you choose to share that is relevant to a loan application.

Identity and supporting documents, collected only after you decide to proceed with a formal application: your Hong Kong Identity Card number and a copy of it, or other identity documents; proof of address for the last three months; proof of income for the last three months (individual institutions or cases may require a longer period); employment proof such as an employer confirmation letter, payslips, tax demand notes or Mandatory Provident Fund (MPF) contribution records; bank account details for loan disbursement; where the application is a credit card balance transfer or card debt consolidation, details of the relevant credit cards (including card numbers) in order to identify the accounts to be settled; where the application is a property mortgage, rates demands, management fee statements, existing mortgage agreements and other property-related documents; and any other documents a financial institution requires for your particular application. We do not request these at the initial enquiry stage. They are used solely to submit your loan application and are then handled under the retention period in section 6.

We will never ask you for: login passwords for online banking, e-wallets or any account; one-time passcodes (OTP); or the CVV/CVC security code on a card, or any card or ATM PIN. We will also never ask you to pay us any fee. If someone claiming to be NORVA asks you for money, a password or an OTP, it is not us — stop the conversation and let us know.

3. Purposes of collection

To respond to your enquiries; to make a preliminary assessment of your situation and identify institutions more likely to approve you; to submit or discuss a loan application on your behalf through a duly appointed referral arrangement; to follow up on your application; and to keep records necessary for legal and commission-settlement purposes. Only with your consent, we may also let you know when a lower-interest option becomes available in future, or help you check whether your credit position has improved (see sections 6 and 9). We collect only what is directly relevant to these purposes.

4. Classes of transferees

We may transfer your personal data to: licensed money lenders and financial institutions in Hong Kong, and their duly appointed referral partners, for assessment of your loan application; legal or regulatory authorities where required by law or court order; and technical service providers — including an overseas AI service provider and a messaging notification service — who handle your data only to process your enquiry. We do not sell, rent or transfer your personal data to anyone for marketing or other unrelated purposes.

5. Whether supply is obligatory

Supply of your data is entirely voluntary. You may decline to answer any question at any time. The only consequence is that, without basic information about your financial situation, we cannot assess which institutions may approve you and cannot approach them on your behalf — meaning we may be unable to help you.

6. Storage and retention

Messages travel through Meta's servers and are governed by Meta's own privacy policy. Our own server does not retain message data — messages are moved off it as soon as they are processed, and anything not yet processed (for example, voice messages) is cleared automatically within 7 days. Long-term records are stored only on devices and encrypted cloud storage controlled by the operator, with access restrictions. Where no loan results, we retain your data for 24 months after your case is closed, then delete it. This period reflects the 2-year prosecution time limit under section 29(3) of the Money Lenders Ordinance and the possibility of commission disputes. Where a loan is approved, transaction and commission records (your preferred name, the institution, loan amount, commission and dates) are kept for at least 7 years as required by section 51C of the Inland Revenue Ordinance; this requirement does not cover copies of your identity documents or your credit report, and your other data is deleted 24 months after your case is closed. We do not keep original credit reports long-term: for an approved loan they are deleted once the commission clawback period (usually 3 months) ends, and otherwise when your case is closed. Key points noted from your credit report — your credit score and grade, repayment propensity percentage, existing debts, recent credit enquiries, and any legal records shown in the report (for example, litigation or bankruptcy) — are kept only with your consent, to help you check later whether your credit position has improved; it is deleted whenever you ask, and in any event together with your other data. If we cease operating this service, we will delete all data obtained through the platforms.

7. Security

Only the operator can access your data — there are no other staff. The technical service providers described in section 4 handle some data (for example, the AI service needs to read message content in order to reply). Before any message is passed to the AI service, your phone, identity card, credit card and bank account numbers are masked on our own computer. Storage devices and cloud accounts are protected by passwords and two-factor authentication. Sensitive personal data is never stored in any public or shared location. We take practicable steps to protect your data, but no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Automated processing and AI

We use automated software, including AI assistance, to receive, organise and reply to messages so that replies are faster. The AI service is provided by an overseas provider, and your message content is sent to its servers for processing. We have switched off the option that allows your data to be used to train AI models; the provider generally retains it for no more than 30 days. However, every substantive decision about your loan application is made by a human, never by software alone. You may request to speak with a human at any time.

9. Direct marketing

We do not use your personal data for direct marketing without your consent. We do not add you to any promotional list, do not send bulk promotional messages, and do not provide your data to third parties for direct marketing. The only exception requires your express consent: before a formal application, we will ask whether we may notify you on WhatsApp if a better-suited option (for example, a lower interest rate) becomes available. Only if you say yes will we use your preferred name and WhatsApp number to let you know when a lower-interest option (one with a lower rate than your current loan) becomes available. You can tell us to stop at any time, free of charge, by WhatsApp, Instagram direct message or email, and we will stop immediately. Otherwise, we reply only to conversations you start, or follow up on an application you have already begun. Should we ever wish to market anything else to you, we will notify you separately in advance, specify the kinds of data and classes of goods or services involved, and obtain your explicit consent first, in accordance with Part 6A of the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance.

10. Your rights: access, correction and deletion

Under the Hong Kong Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486) you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, and to withdraw from further processing at any time.

How to request data deletion: contact us by email at norva888hk@gmail.com or by Instagram direct message to @anorhk1988, stating whether you want access, correction or deletion.

We will respond to access and correction requests within 40 days (the period prescribed by section 19 of the Ordinance), and will complete deletion requests within 30 days and confirm to you. We charge no fee for access, correction or deletion requests. Where the law requires us to retain certain records (for example, a loan application still in progress, or transaction and commission records for an approved loan, which the Inland Revenue Ordinance requires us to keep for 7 years), we will tell you clearly which part cannot be deleted immediately and why.

11. Third-party platforms

When you contact us through Instagram, Facebook Messenger or WhatsApp, your data on those platforms is also governed by Meta's own privacy policy, which is outside the scope of this policy. See the Meta Privacy Policy.

12. Children

Our service is available only to persons aged 18 or above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18, and will delete it immediately if we discover we have.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. If a change is significant, we will notify you through the channel you originally used to contact us.

14. Contact us

For any question, request or complaint about this policy or your personal data, contact the NORVA Privacy Officer at norva888hk@gmail.com or @anorhk1988 on Instagram. If you believe we have handled your personal data in breach of the Ordinance, you may also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data, Hong Kong (PCPD).
